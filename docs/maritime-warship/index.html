FIELD MANUAL

Own the coordinates.

  1. Deploy in secret. Select a vessel, then its starting and ending cells. Highlighted endpoints show legal placements in either direction. Auto-deploy can arrange the full fleet.
  2. Respect the terrain. Ships cannot overlap hazards or each other. Tropical vessels of length four or five need one orthogonal cell of clearance from land.
  3. Fire with intention. Select an unresolved coordinate on your targeting grid and fire. Salvo mode lets you stage up to five targets.
  4. Keep play local. Share the setup code, QR, or invitation once. Each device stores only its own fleet and manually recorded observations. The creator fires first; coordinate turns verbally.
  5. Report as the defender. Select the verbally called coordinates on your lower primary grid and confirm them. The app announces Miss, Hit, or Hit and sunk from your own fleet. Tell the attacker the results out loud. When attacking, manually record what the defender tells you on your targeting grid. Outgoing result choices start blank; incoming outcomes are calculated after coordinate confirmation.
  6. Read the result. × is a hit, • a miss, ⚓ is hit and sunk. Shots at terrain are misses. Sink every opponent vessel to win.
  7. Keep your fleet private. Use Conceal before stepping away. Your opponent’s placements never enter your device.

The setup code contains public rules and terrain only. There is no communication between devices during play. Speak the results out loud, or use your own communication channel. Pass-and-play and automatic resolution of outgoing shots are future options.
